## A simple weekly routine

| Day | Do this | Goal |
|---|---|---|
| Mon | Add 25 new companies to your lead tracker (Google Maps) | 25 new prospects |
| Mon–Tue evening | "After-hours test": call each new company after 6 PM and note what happens | Proof for your emails |
| Tue–Thu | Send 10–20 personal emails per day; call the companies that don't reply | 3–5 conversations |
| Wed–Fri | Visit 3–5 companies in person or at supply houses in the morning | Relationships |
| Fri | Follow up with everyone from last week; update the tracker | Nobody forgotten |

**Numbers to expect when starting out:** roughly 1 in 10–20 contacts leads to a conversation. Roughly 1 in 3–5 conversations leads to a pilot. These are rough guides; your results will vary. Persistence and follow-up matter more than perfect wording.

## Rules to stay on the right side of the law (US)

- **Cold email (CAN-SPAM):**
  - Use your real name and a real postal address (a PO box is fine).
  - Use an honest subject line.
  - Include a simple way to opt out, and honor it quickly.
- **Don't text or auto-dial prospects** without their permission. Texting marketing messages to people who never opted in can break the TCPA, and the fines are large. Email, phone calls you dial by hand, and in-person visits are fine.
- **Never promise results you can't prove.** Say "designed to" or "in our pilot with X we saw…", not "guaranteed".
- Getting paid: see "Getting paid (Venmo)" in `05-pricing-and-pilot.md`.
