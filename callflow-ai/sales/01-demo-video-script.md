# 2-minute demo video script (Loom)

**Tool:** Loom (free), or Zoom recording yourself. Record your screen plus a small camera bubble.
**Before recording:** open your live demo, click "Sign in with demo account", and open **Calls → Call simulator**. Close other tabs and turn off notifications.
**Tip:** record 2–3 takes. Nobody sees the bad ones.

---

**[0:00–0:15] Hook (camera on, simple)**
> "Hi, I'm [Name]. I work with HVAC companies here in [Utah]. Quick question: what happens when someone's AC dies at 9 PM and they call you? For most shops, it's voicemail, and that homeowner calls the next company. Let me show you something that fixes that."

**[0:15–1:05] The emergency call (simulator)**
Click **After-hours AC emergency → Place simulated call**, then click **Say** for each line.
> "This is a homeowner calling after hours. It's 92 degrees and they have a newborn."
> (After the AI flags the emergency) "It recognizes the emergency right away..."
> (After the address) "...checks the address is in your service area..."
> (After the time slots) "...and offers real times from your on-call tech's calendar."
> (After booking) "Booked. The customer got a text confirmation, and on the right you can see the lead, appointment and text were all created automatically. No one on your team had to wake up."

**[1:05–1:35] Estimate follow-up**
Go to **Estimates → EST-1001 → Run automation now**.
> "Second thing: estimates that go quiet. You quote $8,900, and the customer never answers. CallFlow follows up automatically on day 1, 3 and 7. And if they reply STOP or ask for a person, it stops immediately and hands it to you."

**[1:35–1:50] Dashboard**
Open the **Dashboard**.
> "Everything shows up here: calls answered, jobs booked, and revenue recovered."

**[1:50–2:00] Call to action (camera)**
> "I'm offering a free 30-day pilot to a few [Utah] HVAC companies. I set everything up for you. If you'd like to see it with your own business, just reply to my email. Thanks!"

---

**After recording:** give the video a clear title, for example "How [Company] could stop losing after-hours calls". Then copy the share link and use the Loom thumbnail GIF in emails.
**Personalized version (high impact):** for your best prospects, record 20 seconds at the start with their website on screen: "Hi Mike, I was looking at Summit Air's website..."
