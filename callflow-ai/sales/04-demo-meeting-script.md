# The 20-minute demo meeting (Zoom or in person)

**Goal:** agree on a pilot start date. It isn't a tech tour.

**Before the meeting:**
- Open the live demo, sign in with the demo account, and have the call simulator ready. Close other tabs.
- Look at their website and Google reviews. Note their services, hours and service area.
- Have the one-pager and your pilot terms ready to share.

## Agenda

### 1. Questions first (5–7 min): let them talk
- "Walk me through what happens when a call comes in after 6 PM."
- "How many calls do you think you miss in a busy week? What about weekends?"
- "When you send an install estimate, who follows up, and how often?"
- "How are you getting Google reviews today?"
- "What's your average repair ticket? And a typical replacement?"

Write down their numbers. Use them in the ROI calculator on your marketing page.

### 2. Show only what matches their pain (8 min)
- **Missed nights and weekends:** run the **After-hours AC emergency** simulator. Point out the emergency detection, service-area check, real booking, and the SMS confirmation.
- **Estimates go quiet:** open **Estimates → EST-1001 → Run automation now**, then show the reply "STOP" stopping everything.
- **Reviews:** show the Reviews page and the negative-feedback routing to the owner.
- **Always:** end on the **Dashboard**. "This is what you'd see every morning."

### 3. Show the math (2 min)
Open the ROI calculator on your marketing page (`/#roi`) and enter *their* numbers:
> "If this books just two of those after-hours calls a month, that's about $[X]. The plan is $[Y]."

### 4. Offer the pilot (3 min)
> "Here's what I'd suggest: a free 30-day pilot. We start with missed-call text-back and estimate follow-ups. I set it up, you approve every message, and at the end we look at the numbers together. If it's not worth it, you walk away. Fair?"

### 5. Lock the next step (1 min)
> "What day this week works for a 30-minute setup call?"
Put it on the calendar before you hang up or leave.

## After the meeting
- Send email #5 (the recap) the same day.
- Update your lead tracker: stage, next step, date.
- If they said "not now", set a follow-up date and actually follow up.

## In person vs. Zoom
- **In person** builds trust fastest with local trades. Show the demo on a laptop, not a phone.
- **Zoom** is fine for busy owners. Share your screen and keep your camera on.
- Either way: listen more than you talk.
